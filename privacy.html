Privacy Policy

Side Bet Tutorials: Poker for Android · Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

Side Bet Tutorials: Poker keeps your progress on your phone. There are no accounts, and we operate no server that anything is sent to. The app uses the internet for two things only: advertising on the free version, and the Google Play purchase that removes it and unlocks every game.

We do not receive, see or store anything about how you learn or play.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores your course progress, the lesson or practice session in progress, your quiz results, your settings and your statistics in its own private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It opts out of Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so Android does not copy this to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it; because we hold no copy, we cannot restore it.

What is collected, and by whom

Side Bet Tutorials: Poker itself collects nothing. On the free version the app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and that software collects data. If you buy the one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks every game, the advertising software is not started and none of the following applies.

None of this includes your progress or results. Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card details.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Where the law requires it (including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app shows a consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice later at Settings → Privacy choices.

Where ads appear

Ads never appear during a lesson, drill, quiz or practice table. The free version shows a banner on the course home, Statistics and Settings screens and may show one full-screen ad after a session has finished: never in your first two sessions, and no more than one every 90 seconds.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — advertising and Google Play purchases only. The app makes no network request of its own.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob on the free version.
  • Google Play billing — the one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks every game.
  • Vibrate — the optional haptics you can switch off in Settings.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, SMS or phone permissions.

Age

Side Bet Tutorials: Poker teaches casino table games and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Reset statistics on the Progress screen, or uninstall to erase everything on the device.
  • Buy the one-time purchase (Settings → Unlock everything) to stop the advertising software from running.
  • Where a consent choice applies to you, change it at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request, but if you have a question, please write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how data is handled, note it in the app's release notes.
