Help & Support

Side Bet Tutorials: Poker for Android · Last updated 12 November 2026

Get in touch

Write to support@cypresstech.net. Please include the app name, the version from Settings → About, your phone model and what happened.

What does the course cover?

Foundations first: three-card and five-card hand rankings, reading seven cards, what a house edge means and what a hand can cost. Then one module per game: Three Card Poker (play or fold with queen-six-four), Ultimate Texas Hold'em (4x before the flop, 2x on the flop, 1x or fold on the river) and Mississippi Stud (points, then one street at a time). Each module is lessons, drills, a check quiz and a coached practice table that grades every decision.

What is free?

Foundations and Three Card Poker are free, with ads. The one-time purchase removes the ads and unlocks every other game, including games added later. A locked game's first lesson can always be read.

Is this gambling?

No. Practice uses play units with no value. Nothing can be bought, won or cashed out.

Will this strategy make me money?

No. Every game here keeps a house edge with perfect play, and each module states it first. The strategies minimise what a game costs; they do not beat it.

How do I remove the ads and unlock every game?

Settings → Unlock everything. It is a one-time purchase of US$2.99 (shown in your currency before you buy), not a subscription. It removes every ad and opens every game, now and later.

I bought it but still see ads or locked games, or I have a new phone.

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the Google account you bought it with. If the price does not show, check that you are online and that Google Play is up to date.

Do ads ever appear during a lesson?

No. Ads never appear during a lesson, drill, quiz or practice table. The free version shows a banner on the course home, Statistics and Settings screens and may show one full-screen ad after a session has finished.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every lesson, drill, quiz and practice table works with no connection. Ads simply do not appear.

Where is my progress kept?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy, so it does not move to a new phone and is erased if you uninstall.

How do I reset my statistics?

Progress → Reset statistics. It cannot be undone.

How do I change my ad consent?

Where a consent choice applies to you, Settings → Privacy choices opens the form again.
